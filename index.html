Working selection, November 2026

I grew up surrounded by woods in Maryland, somewhere between Annapolis, Baltimore and DC. Home was rough. The woods were where I went to get away.

I was always exploring, and I walked in with no direction in mind. In the woods you can never see everything ahead. Trees cover things. A house on a hill shows through the branches in pieces. Maybe you see a person, and then you don’t.

The trees are anonymous. They stand next to each other and they all look the same. Standing among them, I was one of them. Hidden, but not alone.

As I got older the woods became Baltimore and DC. Later I travelled a lot for work, and it kept going. Tokyo, London, New York, Belgrade. I walked those cities the same way, with no direction in mind, and found the same anonymity. Somewhere to disappear into again.

These photographs come from those walks. People seen from behind, faces turned away or lost in shadow. Windows wet with rain, the water in focus and the world behind it soft. Never the whole thing. Only part of it.

The world is my woods.
